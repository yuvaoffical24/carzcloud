Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book a rental car?
You can book a rental car through our website or mobile app by selecting your location, car type, and rental duration.
What documents do I need to rent a car?
You will need a valid driver's license, a government-issued ID, and a credit or debit card for payment.
Is there a mileage limit on rental cars?
Some rental plans have mileage limits, while others offer unlimited mileage. Check the rental terms before booking.
What happens if I return the car late?
Late returns may be subject to additional charges. Please check our rental policy for more details.
Can I rent a car without a credit card?
In most cases, a credit card is required for security purposes. However, some locations may accept debit cards with additional conditions.
